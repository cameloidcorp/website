What we do

Four sectors. One integrated approach.

We work where people feel the difference first: how children learn, how families stay well, how money moves, and where people gather.

01  Education

Every learner, known and supported.

We help partner schools put each student at the centre, with Canadian credentials, digital resources and teacher development that make personalised learning practical.

For teachers and leaders

Student-centred learning in the age of AI

Hands-on training and coaching that helps teachers and school leaders build classrooms around each learner, using AI to make personal attention possible at scale. Delivered in person and live online by senior educators.

High school

Canadian diploma pathway

Students can earn a Canadian high school diploma alongside their local diploma, taught by Canadian teachers through licensed partners.

Grades 1–8

Digital learning resources

Ontario-aligned digital courses that partner schools can use from the first day, with progress tracked for every child.

All ages

English for every stage

From the early grades through high school credits to professional English for the workplace.

Every student

Learner profiles

Each student begins with a learning and wellbeing profile, so support is planned around the person.

02  Health

Specialist care, shared across many.

Small schools and clinics rarely have the scale to employ specialists of their own. We bring care within reach by sharing it, and by building technology that keeps it personal.

One team of nurses, counsellors and specialists, serving a whole network of schools.

Our shared school health service gives each member school access to expertise it could not support alone, for a simple monthly fee. Much of it is delivered centrally through technology, with qualified local staff on site.

Schools

Shared school health service

Health checks, wellbeing support and specialist referral for every student in the network.

Students

Wellbeing profiles

A clear, private picture of each student’s health and wellbeing, shared with families and the people who support them.

Clinics

Clinic technology

Records, measurement-based care and remote consultation that help small clinics work to Canadian standards.

03  Finance

Capital and enterprise that serve the community.

Local enterprise is how communities fill their own gaps. We help founders build ventures that matter, and help institutions offer financial services that fit local values.

The Founders Program

LearnCanadian college credit courses in business and the student’s chosen field, taught live.
BuildA real venture that answers a real gap in the student’s own community.
LaunchSeeded by family and community, with Cameloid mentors alongside.
GrowPromising ventures may go on to receive investment and support.
Owners and founders

Business management and AI

Practical programmes that help owners of schools, clinics, shops and hotels run and grow their enterprises.

Institutions

Community-aligned finance

Products shaped around each community’s values, including Sharia-compliant options, cooperatives and diaspora investment.

Systems

Payments and lending technology

Systems that let small lenders and institutions serve customers safely, simply and across borders.

04  Hospitality and culture

Places that give a community its pride.

Between home and work, people need somewhere to belong. We help create and run the gathering places, services and cultural life that give a community its identity.

The café, the market, the cultural centre: the third places where community happens.

We work with hosts, retailers and cultural organisations to build welcoming spaces and service people remember.

Service

Service delivery standards

Canadian-benchmarked training in kind, attentive service for hotels, restaurants, shops, clinics and banks.

Places

Community and retail spaces

Planning, technology and operations for the spaces where people meet, shop and spend time together.

Identity

Culture and heritage

Programmes that celebrate local heritage, craft and hospitality traditions.
