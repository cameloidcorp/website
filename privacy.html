Privacy

Privacy notice

How we handle the information you share with us through this website.

This website is operated by Cameloid Corp., Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

What we collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. When you write to us, by email or through our contact form, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address, organisation and message.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you and to follow up on your enquiry. We do not sell or rent it, and we share it only with Cameloid team members or regional partners who need it to respond to you.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence only as long as it is needed for the purpose you contacted us about, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to info@cameloid.com. We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

The Cameloid portal

The Cameloid programme portal has its own privacy terms, which are provided to learners, families and partners when they register.
