Our promise

Why an integrator

The right answer is rarely a single product.

Off-the-shelf systems are built for someone else’s community. We combine proven Canadian and international products, components we build ourselves, and new ventures we help start locally, then deliver them as one programme that fits.

Buy, build or incubate: usually all three.

Meeting communities where they are

Every service has its own starting point.

We assess each service in each community on a five-stage scale, then offer only what fits that stage. What works in one city may not suit the next.

01VoidThe service is missing or out of reach.
02NascentEarly efforts with limited capacity.
03DevelopingWorking, but uneven in quality.
04EstablishedReliable and widely used.
05BenchmarkA model that others learn from.

A clear path from first look to lasting change.

Illuminate · Architect · Orchestrate · Transform!!

For partner institutions

We become your technology office.

Most schools, clinics and small lenders are too small to hire a chief technology officer or run a major systems project on their own. As your partner, Cameloid takes on that role, with little or no upfront cost, and is paid as the improvements begin to earn.
