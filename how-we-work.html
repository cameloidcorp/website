How we work

A clear path from first look to lasting change.

Every community is different, so every programme is too. What stays the same is how we work: listen first, design carefully, deliver together, and measure honestly.

Our method

Illuminate. Architect. Orchestrate. Transform!!

Four stages, repeated in every community and every service we work on.

01

Illuminate

We start by understanding the community as it is.

  • Community readiness audit across 40 criteria
  • Each service placed on our five-stage scale
  • Conversations with families, institutions and leaders
02

Architect

We design a programme that fits, combining what exists with what is missing.

  • Proven products, bought and adapted
  • Cameloid-built platforms and tools
  • Local ventures incubated to fill the gaps
03

Orchestrate

We deliver with local partners, never around them.

  • Canadian specialists supporting local teams
  • Shared services delivered centrally where possible
  • One portal for learners, families and partners
04

Transform!!

We measure, improve and scale what works.

  • Progress measured against Canadian benchmarks
  • Partners join the Lodestar network
  • Proven models carried to the next community

Five stages of maturity

We offer what fits, not what we happen to sell.

A community can have a benchmark school and no clinic at all. We assess each service on its own and match our work to its stage.

VoidThe service is missing or out of reach.
NascentEarly efforts with limited capacity.
DevelopingWorking, but uneven in quality.
EstablishedReliable and widely used.
BenchmarkA model that others learn from.

Working with Cameloid

Five ways to work together.

We choose the right model for each place: through partners large or small, through our own presence, or a mix of both.

Technology partnership

Your CTO and CIO, without the cost of hiring one.

We act as your institution’s technology office: we assess your strengths and gaps, plan improvements tied to new revenue, and put the right systems in place, whether from the market or built by us. There is little or no upfront fee; we are paid as the improvements begin to earn.

Shared services

Specialists, shared across a network

Health, learning support and back-office services that many small institutions can share for a monthly fee, delivered centrally with local staff on site.

Programmes

Programmes through your institution

Canadian programmes for your students, staff or community, run in your facilities with your team, and a share of the proceeds for you.

New institutions

Building what is missing, together

Where a community needs a new school, clinic or centre, we help plan, build and run it with local investors and partners.

Assessment

Community readiness audit

A clear, evidence-based picture of a community’s services and where investment will do the most good. Often the first step.

The Lodestar network

We earn when you earn.

Our partners join a network of institutions that share ideas, specialists, resources and, where it helps, financing. The scale of the network gives each member buying power, supplier support and access to expertise no single institution could secure alone.

Every partnership starts with a conversation.

Tell us about your community and your institution. We will listen first.
